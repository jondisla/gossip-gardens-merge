# Repair spots (6 images) -> gossip-gardens/art/scenes/
Each area has 2 spots. A spot is one phone-shaped image with 6 repairs in it. The camera pans or zooms from spot 1 to
spot 2 when the sixth repair of spot 1 is done. The repair pieces (gate, lantern, roof...) are drawn on top as
separate images, so each scene must have six flat, empty, bare patches of ground for them.

How to use
- Start a NEW chat for every spot. Paste the STYLE line, then the subject line below.
- For the same art style, attach one approved image and add: `use this only for the art style, ignore its layout`.
- Ask for the biggest size your Gemini offers (4K if there is a choice). Phone-shaped 9:16 (not narrower).
- If it draws objects, text or white boxes, add: `the six patches must be completely bare, nothing on them`.

STYLE (spots): `Mobile game scene, top-down isometric view from high above, clean hand-painted cartoon style, soft warm light, sharp crisp details, one consistent colour palette. Exactly six separate flat bare patches of ground, each large, smooth and empty, spread evenly across the picture with space between them. No people, animals, signs, lamps, furniture, text, outlines, numbers or markers. Portrait 9:16, very high resolution.`
